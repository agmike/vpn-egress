Backup

One file with everything set here: exits and their keys, the pipe's key and peers, routing, DNS and the allowance. Not in it: the traffic counts, and settings that come from the router's environment (pipe port, addresses, endpoint). Peers keep their configs after a restore.

Download a backup

With a passphrase the file is encrypted (AES-256-GCM, scrypt); without one it holds the private keys in plain text. There is no way to recover a forgotten passphrase.
