List format

One entry per line:

dmm.co.jpthe domain and all its subdomains (*. is optional)
jpa whole top-level domain: everything under .jp
JPJapan's IP ranges (GeoIP; capitals matter: jp is the TLD)
NETFLIXa service's IP ranges: CLOUDFLARE, CLOUDFRONT, FACEBOOK, FASTLY, GOOGLE, NETFLIX, TELEGRAM, TOR, TWITTER
AS2906a network's announced ranges (ASN)
https://…/list.txta remote list of domains or ranges, refreshed daily (plain, hosts, dnsmasq, RPZ or JSON)
203.0.113.0/24an IPv4 or IPv6 address or range
# streaming CDNsa comment, on its own line or after an entry

Put specific lists above broad ones: services and domains before countries. Domains route by the addresses they resolve to, so a site may need a reload after it's added, and other sites sharing a CDN address with a listed one go the same way; to block a name itself, use DNS. GeoIP is where addresses are registered: anycast CDNs (Cloudflare, Google) count as US wherever they serve from.
