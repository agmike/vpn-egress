List format

One entry per line:

dmm.co.jpthe domain and all its subdomains
*.dmm.co.jpthe same (the *. is optional)
jpa whole top-level domain: everything under .jp
203.0.113.7one IPv4 address
203.0.113.0/24an IPv4 range
2001:db8::/32an IPv6 address or range
# streaming CDNsa comment, on its own line or after an entry

Domains route by the addresses they resolve to, so a site may need a reload after it's added, and other sites sharing a CDN address with a listed one go the same way.
